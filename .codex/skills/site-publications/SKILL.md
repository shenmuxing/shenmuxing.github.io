# Site Publications

## Workflow

1. Inspect `_bibliography/papers.bib`, `_pages/publications.md`, `_config.yml` `scholar`, and `_data/coauthors.yml` before changing publication behavior.
2. Remove or hide sample Einstein bibliography data from the public site unless it is intentionally kept as an example.
3. Prefer BibTeX fields already supported by al-folio: `abbr`, `abstract`, `arxiv`, `bibtex_show`, `code`, `doi`, `html`, `pdf`, `preview`, `selected`, `slides`, `supp`, `video`, `website`.
4. Keep publication copy factual. Do not invent papers, venues, links, awards, or citation counts.
5. Build after edits because BibTeX or Jekyll Scholar failures can break the site.

## Public Display

- `_pages/publications.md` renders `{% bibliography %}`.
- `_config.yml` controls author highlighting, badges, thumbnails, and filtered BibTeX keywords.
- `_data/citations.yml` is generated by citation-update workflows; do not hand-edit unless debugging that workflow.
- `_data/coauthors.yml` maps BibTeX author names to profile URLs and affiliations.

## Empty or Pending Publications

If no verified publications are ready, keep the publications page honest:

- disable navigation, or
- replace the bibliography body with a short English placeholder, or
- keep the page visible with no fake entries.

For detailed BibTeX syntax reminders, consult `.github/instructions/bibtex-bibliography.instructions.md` only when needed.
